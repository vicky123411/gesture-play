# Gesture Play 👋

A gesture game for children. Lord Krishna (奎師那) guides you, and you play with your hands in front of the camera.

## ▶️ Play now

**[Play Gesture Play in your browser](https://vicky123411.github.io/gesture-play/)**: no download, no install.

Scan with your phone camera:

<img src="qr.png" alt="QR code to play Gesture Play" width="220">

## How to play

1. Open the link and wait for the loading bar to reach 100%. The first visit takes a little longer (about 50 MB).
2. Tap **Allow** when the browser asks to use the camera.
3. On a phone, turn it **sideways**.
4. Show your hand to the camera:
   - ✋ **Open hand**: move
   - ✊ **Fist**: grab
   - 🖐️ **Hold still** on a button: press it
5. No camera? Just **touch the screen** or use the **mouse**. The game switches to touch right away.

## Games

| Game | What to do |
|---|---|
| 🍎 Fruit Picking | Catch the falling fruit |
| ⭐ Catch Stars | Touch the stars as they fall |
| ✋ Gesture Match | Copy the hand shape on the card |
| 🎯 Hand Move | Move your hand to the glowing rings |

## Languages

- 繁體中文 (Taiwan)
- English

You can change the language on the home screen.

## Works best on

- The newest **Chrome**, **Edge** or **Safari**, on a phone, tablet or computer.
- Hand tracking works best in good light, with your whole hand in view of the camera.
