# Gesture Play 👋

A gesture game for children. Lord Krishna (奎師那) guides you, and you play with your hands in front of the camera.

**Play in the browser:** https://vicky123411.github.io/gesture-play/

- Allow the camera when the browser asks.
- On a phone, turn it sideways.
- Hand tracking works best in the newest Chrome, Edge or Safari. You can also play with touch or the mouse.

Built with Unity 6 (Web build).
